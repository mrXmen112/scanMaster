Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version. Your documents are processed on your device. We do not read, store, or look at the contents of your files. We store your email address and basic profile details only if you choose to create an account, and we never sell your data.

Information We Collect

1. Documents and files

Documents you open, scan, convert or edit are processed locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers for the core tools — merging, splitting, compressing, rotating, page extraction and most conversions all run entirely on your phone or tablet.

If you explicitly use a feature that requires analysis (for example AI Summary, Handwriting to Text or Business Card Scanner), the specific page you choose to analyse is sent to our AI processor for that request only, as described below. Nothing else is transmitted.

2. Camera access

Camera permission is requested only when you start a scan. Images captured stay on your device. We do not receive them unless you deliberately send a page to an AI feature.

3. Storage and file access

File access permission lets you pick documents to work on. ScanMaster only reads the files you select and writes output back to a location you choose.

4. Account information

If you create an account, we store:

DataPurpose
Email addressTo sign you in and, if you opt in, send service messages
Display nameShown on your profile
Avatar URLShown on your profile, if you use a third-party sign-in
User IDInternal identifier linking your data to your account
Feature usage countersTo apply free-usage limits and prevent abuse

You can use most of ScanMaster without an account. Account data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database and is protected by Row Level Security, so your rows are only ever visible to your own signed-in account.

5. AI processing

AI features route your request through our server to an AI model provider. Only the content you submit for that one request is transmitted. Prompts and responses are not used to train third-party models, and we do not retain a copy after the response is returned to you.

6. Usage limits

We track how many AI operations you have used so free limits work. This is a counter only — it contains no information about your documents.

7. Advertising

ScanMaster is supported by ads. We use Google AdMob to display them. AdMob may share your advertising identifier and approximate location with Google and its partners to serve and measure ads. We do not receive your name or email from AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings, and in-app purchase removes ads.

8. No payment data

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, billing address, or payment credentials.

9. Permissions used

PermissionWhy
CameraScanning documents and photographing business cards
Files and storageOpening documents you select and saving results
MicrophoneOnly if you use speech-to-text
NotificationsOptional; only if you enable reminders

How We Use Information

  • To provide the app's features and keep your account working
  • To apply free-usage limits and prevent automated abuse
  • To serve advertising
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising profiling or profiling for advertising.

Data Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play — payments and distribution
  • AI model provider — only for the single request you submit

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose data if legally required, such as in response to a valid legal process.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Account data is kept while your account exists
  • Deleting your account removes your profile and stored files from our database
  • Documents you process stay on your device and are not retained by us
  • AI request contents are discarded after the response is returned

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for ads

You can delete your account and its data directly in the app under Profile → Delete Account. For anything else, contact us and we will action it within a reasonable time.

Data Security

Data is transmitted over TLS. Access to the database is protected by Row Level Security, so each signed-in user can only ever read and write their own rows — not anyone else's. Documents you process never leave your device unless you send one to an AI feature. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable and appropriate safeguards.

Children's Privacy

ScanMaster is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Responsibility

You are responsible for the documents you process and for making sure you have the right to use them. Do not use ScanMaster for unlawful material.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. If we make a material change we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use ScanMaster after a change means you accept the updated policy.
